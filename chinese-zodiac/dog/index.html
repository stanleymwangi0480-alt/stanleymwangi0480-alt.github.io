Chinese Zodiac: The Dog

Dear Doggie,

Man's best buddy truly: you are truthful, loyal, and real. You provide true worth back for love. You are a nice pal—when you're feeling happy. To meet you is to like you, unless you toss out a mean remark or shoot quick jabs at us clueless folks nearby. You have a loud yap, Doggie, that makes even your worst chomp feel mild.

But you kindly fix that. You trust in rightness for everyone and gladly pick up good fights and carry big loads against wrong rules. You honor old ways and prize respect. You find true joy in aiding an older person cross a crowded road. In life's grand cowboy tale, you surely sport a white hat.
