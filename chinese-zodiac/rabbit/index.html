Chinese Zodiac: The Rabbit

Ah, Virtuous Cat/Rabbit, you seek harmony. You are cautious and practice discretion in all matters. When you sense a conflict approaching, you quickly retreat and hide. Distinguished, diplomatic, and well-mannered, you are also as nervous as a cat. Culture and refinement provide you with comfort. You do not dislike money either; you instinctively know how to earn and preserve your fortune without ostentation or display. You prefer traditional antique furnishings over contemporary chrome and glass. You are a soft-stepping go-getter—ambitious but never aggressive, strong but never rough.

Your particular brand of wisdom suggests, "He who fights and runs away lives to fight another day." You are hypersensitive—even squeamish. You are somewhat of a hypochondriac: an innocent hangnail on your big toe alarms you. "Call an ambulance, get me to the emergency room!" Chronic illnesses—migraine, asthma, hay fever, lower back pain, warts, hives—you have experienced them all. Purchase a large house in the countryside, then fill it with brilliant, well-behaved children and pets. Choose a partner from among gentle Goats, scrupulous Pigs, or loyal Dogs. If you are feeling generous, remember that Snakes are delighted to recline on Cats' velvet sofas. Avoid Tigers, Roosters, and Dragons; their relentless flamboyance threatens your tranquil existence. You plan your life to proceed smoothly, and usually it does. But should spontaneity enter the picture and events go awry, you panic. Take my advice and relax, not only on yourself but, more especially, on the rest of us.
